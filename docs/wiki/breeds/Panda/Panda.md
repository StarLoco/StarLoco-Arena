# The Pandawa Pint

The panda is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in picking up and throwing characters. Initial stats: [HP:](../../mechanics/HealthPoints.md) 75 [Initiative:](../../mechanics/Initiative.md) 30

## Primary uses

* Very potent as control units to put allies in the right position. For example they can throw mutilation-boosted iops into range.
* Also very good at protecting high damage/low hp characters by simply picking them up. In this way they simply act as a punch-bag. Their relative high HP suit this purpose good.
* In a combination with the above, they are often used to give increased range.
* Very frequently seen used in trap-teams where the panda uses a combination of the spells [Karzam](./spells/Karzam.md) and [Chamrak](./spells/Chamrak.md) to throw enemies into traps, or in a variant, throw an ally trap-sram into range which later on will be switched by a [sacrier](../Sacrier/Sacrier.md).
* Also very useful as a counter against trap-teams. Having used [Stabilization](./spells/Stabilization.md), a panda holding a character can not in any way be lured into a trap.
* [Explosive Flask](./spells/ExplosiveFlask.md) is alongside with the [xelor](../Xelor/Xelor.md)'s [Temporal Dust](../Xelor/spells/TemporalDust.md) the most dangerous area damage spell. The flask being very expensive is probably why it is seldom seen used. Also the [xelor](../Xelor/Xelor.md) has other spells such as [Counter](../Xelor/spells/Counter.md) and [Slow Down](../Xelor/spells/SlowDown.md) that can be used together with the area damage spell, while the panda has very few spells that work good with the flask.
