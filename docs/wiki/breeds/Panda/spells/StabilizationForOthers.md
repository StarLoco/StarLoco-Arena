# Stabilization for Others

This buff makes target ally immune against many movement altering spells.

**Class:** Panda

**Cost:** 150K

**AP:** 3

**Target:** Non-Self

**Range:** 1 to 1

**Duration:** Game

**Recast:** No

**Effect:** Prevents an ally from being pushed or pulled.

---

**Notes**
- Only works on allies, not the caster.
- Neither cards nor spells can push/pull a stabilized character.
- Stabilization hinders getting moved from the following spells: [Fear](../../Sram/spells/Fear.md), [Tricky Blow](../../Sram/spells/TrickyBlow.md), [Attraction](../../Sacrier/spells/Attraction.md), [Alcoholic Blow](./AlcoholicBlow.md) and [Retreat Arrow](../../Cra/spells/RetreatArrow.md). (Spell damage is still taken.)
- Stabilization does not prevent a character from [Karzam](./Karzam.md), [Karcham](./Karcham.md), [Assault](../../Sacrier/spells/Assault.md) or [Transposition](../../Sacrier/spells/Transposition.md).
