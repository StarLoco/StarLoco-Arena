# Pandatak

This is a damage spell which affects an area.

**Class**: [Panda](../Panda.md)

**Cost**: 300K

**AP**: 6

**Target**: Enemy

**Range**: 2 to 5 in Line

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: 9 (Earth) dmg in area

**Critical Effect**: 15 (Earth) dmg in area

**Area**: Target tile plus the next two tiles in the targeted line.

---

**Notes**

* Area affected is target tile plus the next two tiles that lies in targeted line. Actual reach of spell is with other words 7.
* It is possible to hit tiles behind trees and other obstacles with this spells.
* See [this page](../../../mechanics/Damage.md) for a comparison chart of all damage spells.
