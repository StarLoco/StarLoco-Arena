# Life Theft

This is a life stealing spell.

**Class**: [Sram](../Sram.md)

**Cost**: 250K

**AP**: 2

**Target**: Any

**Range**: 1 to 1

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: Steals 5 (Neutral) dmg

**Critical Effect**: Steals 8 (Neutral) dmg

---

**Notes**

* One get has much life as damage inflicted. If target has [resistance](../../../mechanics/Resistance.md) or using [damage reflection](../../../mechanics/DamageReflect.md), one will get less [HP](../../../mechanics/HealthPoints.md) back.
* It is possible to steal from both enemies as well as allies including summons such as the sram's [double](./Double.md).
* See [this page](../../../mechanics/Damage.md) for a comparison chart of all damage spells.
