# Fear

This spell pushes back any ally, enemy or summon one tile.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Sram |
| **Cost** | 200K |
| **AP** | 1 |
| **Target** | Any |
| **Range** | 1 to 1 |
| **Recast** | Yes |
| **Effect** | Pushes back target one tile |

**Notes**
- Fearing a character into a wall will hurt him 6 damage. Fearing someone 6 times into a wall hurt him 36 damage.
- Fearing someone into another character will cause both to lose 3 damage each.
- Also very useful to break out of [dodge rolls](../../../rules/Dodge.md) or to push someone into a [trap](./LethalTrap.md) or tiles that are about to [drop](../../../mechanics/TileDrop.md).
