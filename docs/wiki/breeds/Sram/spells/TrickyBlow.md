# Tricky Blow

This is a damage spell which also pushes back target three tiles.

**Class**: Sram
**Cost**: 150K
**AP**: 4
**Target**: Enemy
**Range**: 1 to 1
**Recast**: Yes
**Effect**: 8 air dmg, moves target 3 tiles away
**Critical Effect**: 10 air dmg, moves target 3 tiles away

---

**Notes**
- If the target is pushed into an obstacle, it takes extra damage. The exact amount can be found on the Damage page.
- See the Damage page for a comparison chart of all damage spells.
