# Sram's Shadow

The sram is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in damaging opponent by [pushing](../../mechanics/Damage.md#push-damage), [back-stabbing](../../mechanics/Damage.md#backstab-damage) and luring opponents into its [trap](./spells/LethalTrap.md). Initial stats: [HP:](../../mechanics/HealthPoints.md) 70 [Initiative:](../../mechanics/Initiative.md) 40

## Primary uses

- Not many srams out there does not use [Fear](./spells/Fear.md). It is probably the single most useful multi-purpose spell in the game which is probably why the sram is a very popular class.
- The sram has the most powerful life-stealing spell: [Life Theft](./spells/LifeTheft.md).
- Many teams use the sram only for its spell [Diversion](./spells/Diversion.md). This spell has the ability to turn around opponents to gain a substantial back-stab damage boost.
- The sram's only summon [Double](./spells/Double.md) is one of the most powerful summon spell there is in the whole game. It is fast, has lots of HP, and is easy to resummon. Even though it does not make any damage itself, one can use it to [block](../../mechanics/Block.md), to steal life, or to cause push damage with fear by placing it between you and the victim just to mention a few strategies.
- The [Lethal Trap](./spells/LethalTrap.md) can kill any character in one turn. Whole strategies can be designed with this spell in mind. Those teams are commonly referred to as _trapteams_.
