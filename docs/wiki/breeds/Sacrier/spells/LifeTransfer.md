# Life Transfer

This spell heals nearby allies while hurting the caster.

**Class**: [Sacrier](../Sacrier.md)

**Cost**: 200K

**AP**: 6

**Target**: Self

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: 30 (Neutral) dmg to self, heals 30 to targets nearby

---

**Notes**

* Area affected is all tiles within distance 2 from caster tile. Totally 12 tiles affected.
* Enemies does not get healed by life transfer.
* Even if sacrier has less than 30 HP, he can still cast life transfer, allies will get full 30 HP heal even if sacrier dies.
