# Sacrier's Foot

This is a damage spell which also removes 1 range from target.

**Class:** Sacrier

**Cost:** 150K

**AP:** 3

**Target:** Enemy

**Range:** 1 to 1

**Duration:** 1 Round

**Recast:** Yes

**Effect:** 7 earth dmg, -1 range

**Critical Effect:** 12 earth dmg, -1 range
