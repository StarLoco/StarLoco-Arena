# Sacrifice

This spell gives target a sacrifice buff bound to caster.

**Class:** Sacrier

**Cost:** 200K

**AP:** 4

**Target:** Non-Self

**Range:** 1 to 5

**Round:** 4 Rounds

**Recast:** 5 Rounds

**Effect:** Target get a swap buff bound to caster

---

**Notes**
- One of the more complicated spells. When target is hurt, the position of the caster and the target is swapped and caster takes all the damage while the target remain unharmed.
- All damage including [Mutilation](../../Iop/spells/Mutilation.md), [Punishment](./Punishment.md), damage from [AP poisoning](../../Ecaflip/spells/Roulette.md), etc are swapped.
- The swap works even if the sacrier and the one with the sacrifice buff are far apart. (Great way for fast teleport!)
- If casting an area spell on both the sacrier and the one with the sacrifice buff, the sacrier will take double damage.
- Chained sacrifice is possible, though only one swap will take place. For example if A sacrifice B who sacrifice C who sacrifice A, the following will happen:
  1. Hitting A: C takes damage, C swap with A.
  2. Hitting B: A takes damage, A swap with B.
  3. Hitting C: B takes damage, B swap with C.
- With sacrifice it is possible to do a lot of crazy stuff. For example you can sacrifice an iop, let him jump into enemy territory and mutilate, and suddenly you got your sacrier there with full AP ready to do some damage.
