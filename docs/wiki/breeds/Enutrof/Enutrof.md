# Enutrof's Finger

The Enutrof is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in altering the [MP](../../mechanics/MovementPoints.md) of characters. Initial stats: HP: 65, Initiative: 45

## Primary uses

- The Enutrof has some various buffs and debuffs that adds and removes [MP](../../mechanics/MovementPoints.md)s. Among them the spell [Acceleration](./spells/Acceleration.md) is most often seen used.
- [Bribery](./spells/Bribery.md) is a spell one can use to keep an opponent at bay. Very useful against high geared super-characters.
- The Enutrof has the spell [Prime of Life](./spells/PrimeOfLife.md) that is unique in that it helps all other enutrofs. No other spell has this class exclusive behavior. The combination [Prime of Life](./spells/PrimeOfLife.md) and multiple enutrofs can potentially be deadly.
- Enutrofs are the only ones that can dispel opponents with the aid of [Ghostly Shovel](./spells/GhostlyShovel.md).
