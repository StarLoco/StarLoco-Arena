# Prime of Life

This buff increases the damage of all enutrof allies for 1 round.

**Class**: [Enutrof](../Enutrof.md)

**Cost**: 200K

**AP**: 4

**Target**: All enutrof allies

**Duration**: 1 Round

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: +3 dmg of all elements

---

**Notes**

* All enutrof allies will benifit from the buff including self.
* The buff will last one round, thus it can be used the next turn. (Read more about duration [here](../../../mechanics/Duration.md).)
