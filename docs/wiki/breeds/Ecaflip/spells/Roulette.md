# Roulette

This spell makes everybody hurt every time they use AP for 2 rounds.

**Class:** Ecaflip

**Cost:** 200K

**AP:** 2

**Target:** All

**Duration:** 2 Rounds

**Recast:** 5 Rounds

**Effect:** Poisoning of AP: 2 neutral dmg lost for each AP used

---

**Notes**
- Work as the [event card](../../../rules/EventCards.md) "Appearance of the Goddess Pandawa".
- Is taking effect immediately. Caster will lose 4 HP.
- The ecaflip itself will be poisoned by this spell 3 rounds.
- The poison is dealt out after each spell is cast. So it is possible for example to have 3 HP and cast [Revitalizing Word](../../Eniripsa/spells/RevitalizingWord.md).
