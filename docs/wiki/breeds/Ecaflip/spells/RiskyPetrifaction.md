# Risky Petrifaction

This spell causes each characters to be immobilized with 20% chance.

**Class:** Ecaflip

**Cost:** 200K

**AP:** 5

**Target:** All

**Duration:** 1 Round

**Recast:** Yes

**Effect:** 20% chance of immobilization

---

**Notes**
- See [this page](../../../mechanics/Immobilization.md) for more info about immobilization.
- The ecaflip himself can be [immobilized](../../../mechanics/Immobilization.md), and if so, he loses both current turn and the next.
