# Perception

This spell makes all invisible characters in targeted area become visible.

**Class**: [Ecaflip](../Ecaflip.md)

**Cost**: 100K

**AP**: 3

**Target**: Any

**Range**: 1 to 4

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: Detects invisible characters in area

**Area**: (see area diagram on original page)

**Notes**

* Invisible allies in target area will also become visible to the enemy.
