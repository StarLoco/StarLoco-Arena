# All or Nothing

This spell causes damage and healing to all characters.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Ecaflip |
| **Cost** | 100K |
| **AP** | 4 |
| **Target** | All |
| **Recast** | No |
| **Effect** | 5D6 (Neutral) dmg, heals 4D6 |

**Notes**

- As with Heads or Tails, the damage is dealt first and the healing afterwards. If target dies from the damage, no healing is taking place.
- Well suited for finishing off enemys with low HP or cleaning the board of low HP summons such as tofus or madolls.
- This spell will in average do 17.5 dmg and heal 14 dmg, an average loss of 3.5 dmg.
