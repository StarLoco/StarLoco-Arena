# Ecalip's Coin

The ecaflip is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in the random aspects of the game like [critical hits](../../mechanics/CriticalHits.md) and [misses](../../mechanics/CriticalHits.md#critical-failures). Initial stats: HP: 75, Initiative: 35

## Primary uses

- Often used as damage dealers ([Heads or Tails](./spells/HeadsOrTails.md) being particularly popular) with items that increase damage. One reason being the good range and another that the spells deal higher average damage against character with low HP since no healing occurs when the target dies.
- The ability to make opponent critical miss a lot with the aid of [Rotten Luck](./spells/RottenLuck.md) is in many cases as effective as the xelor's [Slow Down](../Xelor/spells/SlowDown.md), though the effect is not predictable.
- [Roulette](./spells/Roulette.md), [All or Nothing](./spells/AllOrNothing.md) and [Risky Petrifaction](./spells/RiskyPetrifaction.md) are spells that affect all players, including caster. Hard to use but potentially useful in a careful thought out strategy. [Clover](./spells/Clover.md), [Feline Leap](./spells/FelineLeap.md) and [Perception](./spells/Perception.md) are also useful spells, but seldom seen in favor of other spells.
