# Time Theft

This spell steals 2 AP from target.

**Class**: Xelor
**Cost**: 300K
**AP**: 5
**Target**: Enemy
**Range**: 1 to 3
**Duration**: 1 Round
**Recast**: Yes
**Effect**: -2 AP target, +2 AP self
**Critical Effect**: -3 AP target, +3 AP self

---

**Notes**
- Together with Slow Down this spell can cause a lot of headache for the opponent.
