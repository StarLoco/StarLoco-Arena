# Devotion

This buff gives target 2 AP for one turn.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Xelor |
| **Cost** | 200K |
| **AP** | 4 |
| **Target** | Non-Self |
| **Range** | 1 to 2 in Line |
| **Duration** | 1 Round |
| **Recast** | 1 per Turn |
| **Effect** | +2 AP |

**Notes**
- Typically used to boost an already powerful character.
- Particularly useful on characters with 4 AP spells and high damage gear.
- Big weakness of spell is if you separate the xelor with its target, you will naturally not get the boost.
