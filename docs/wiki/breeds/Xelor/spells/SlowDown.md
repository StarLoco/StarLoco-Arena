# Slow Down

This debuff removes 1 [AP](../../../mechanics/ActionPoints.md) from target.

**Class:** Xelor

**Cost:** 200K

**AP:** 1

**Target:** Enemy

**Range:** 1 to 6

**Duration:** 1 Round

**Recast:** Once Each

**Effect:** -1 AP

**Critical Effect:** -2 AP

---

**Notes**
- Very useful against characters with 6 AP spells/weapons.
- Extremely useful against [gobballs](../../Osamoda/spells/SummoningOfGobball.md) and other summons with 6 AP spells. One xelor with [Slow Down](./SlowDown.md) can nullify up to 6 gobballs, making them useless.
