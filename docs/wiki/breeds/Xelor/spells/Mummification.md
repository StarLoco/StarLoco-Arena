# Mummification

This buff gives target immunity to MP/AP losses but permanently removes 1 MP.

**Class**: [Xelor](../Xelor.md)

**Cost**: 100K

**AP**: 6

**Target**: Self

**Duration**: Game

**Recast**: No

**Effect**: -1 MP, MP/AP loss insensibility

---

**Notes**

* The -1 MP penalty as well as the buff is permanent for rest of game.
* Mummification nullify following spells:
  1. [Bribery](../../Enutrof/spells/Bribery.md)
  2. [Clumsiness](../../Enutrof/spells/Clumsiness.md)
  3. [Mass Clumsiness](../../Enutrof/spells/MassClumsiness.md)
  4. [Slow Down](./SlowDown.md)
  5. [Time Theft](./TimeTheft.md)
  6. [Cut](../../Iop/spells/Cut.md)
  7. [Frozen Arrow](../../Cra/spells/FrozenArrow.md)
  8. Madoll's [Irritation](../../Sadida/spells/Madoll.md)
  9. Prespic's [Mockery](../../Osamoda/spells/SummoningOfPrespic.md)
  10. Crackler's [Crushing](../../Osamoda/spells/SummoningOfCrackler.md)
