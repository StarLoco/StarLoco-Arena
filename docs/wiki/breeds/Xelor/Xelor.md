# Xelor's Sandglass

The xelor is one of many classes. It specializes in altering the AP of characters. Initial stats: HP: 65, Initiative: 65

## Primary uses

- Many teams uses xelors only for Devotion as an AP booster to other classes.
- The xelor is exceptionally good at shattering many carefully designed strategies. Slow Down is a very powerful spell since it allows for a small AP cost to counter strategies that uses all the APs to be effective. For example trap teams or summon spams. Mummification is another spell that nullify a handful of spells and strategies.
- Temporal Dust is alongside with the panda's Explosive Flask the most dangerous area damage spells.
- Counter is a cheap but extremely useful buff that most xelors usually include in their arsenal.
