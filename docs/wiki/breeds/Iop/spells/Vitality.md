# Vitality

This spell increases the life of the caster.

**Class**: Iop
**Cost**: 300K
**AP**: 6
**Target**: Self
**Duration**: Game
**Recast**: No
**Effect**: +30 life
**Critical Effect**: +40 life

---

**Notes**
- Caster gain both extra maximum HP and extra current HP.
- This spell is dispellable but the iop will keep the extra HPs until he is healed after which his HPs will be reset to max should he have more current HPs than his max HPs.
