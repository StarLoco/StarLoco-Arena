# Bravery Guide

This buff increases damage of characters nearby.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Class** | Iop |
| **Cost** | 200K |
| **AP** | 2 |
| **Target** | Allies |
| **Range** | 0 to 1 |
| **Duration** | 1 Round |
| **Recast** | 1 per Turn |
| **Effect** | +3 dmg of all elements in area |

**Area**: Target tile and neighboring tiles (see original page for diagram).

**Notes**

- Spell also affect enemy.
- One need not to stay in the area to keep the buff.
- Caster iop will gain double benifit of the buff. Suppose he use the combo Bravery Guide + Celestial Sword every turn. He will then make 8 + 3 + 3 = 14 damage every turn since he both get buff from the bravery guide he just cast but also the one he cast one turn ago.
