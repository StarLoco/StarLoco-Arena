# Mutilation

This spell increases the damage dealt by caster but at the same time hurt the caster.

**Class**: [Iop](../Iop.md)

**Cost**: 100K

**AP**: 1

**Target**: Self

**Duration**: 1 Round

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: +2 dmg of all elements, inflict 5 (Neutral) dmg to caster

---

**Notes**

* Boosted damage last one round. This means the iop can use the damage boost from mutilation two rounds, the round he cast it and his next turn. (See note about [duration](../../../mechanics/Duration.md).)
* Damage boost is accumalative. Casting mutilation 9 times yields +18 damage.
* The spell [Immunity](../../Feca/spells/Immunity.md) or [Truce](../../Feca/spells/Truce.md) prevent the iop from taking any damage from mutilation, but he still get the damage boost.
* If a sacrier has cast the spell [Sacrifice](../../Sacrier/spells/Sacrifice.md) on the iop then when the iop cast mutilation the sacrier will take the 5 damage and switch places with the iop.
