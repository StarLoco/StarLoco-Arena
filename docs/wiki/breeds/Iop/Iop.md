# Iop

## Iop's Heart

The iop is one of many classes. It specializes in dealing and boosting damage. Initial stats: HP: 75, Initiative: 35

## Primary uses

- Often used as a primary damage dealer. Often with the aid of Mutilation and a lot of extra AP and/or HP.
- Iops are also frequently used as a damage booster of some other characters with Bravery Guide and/or Increase. The other characters often being (but not necessarily) cras or ecaflips.
- With the spell Jump, the iop becomes many times a very versatile, mobile unit. This effect is often boosted with an increased range.
