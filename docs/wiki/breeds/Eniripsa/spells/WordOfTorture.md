# Word of Torture

This spell increases maximum HP of all allies while hurting caster.

**Class**: Eniripsa
**Cost**: 300K
**AP**: 6
**Target**: All Allies
**Duration**: Game
**Recast**: No
**Effect**: 40 neutral to self, +10 life to all allies

---

**Notes**
- Both maximum HP and actual HP is raised by 10.
- Any friendly summoned creature will also get the HP boost.
- If the eniripsa has less than 40 HP and thus will die, the others will still gain the boost but only as long as they are not healed (see next note).
- If the eniripsa is killed or dispelled, character will lose their 10 extra max HPs but will still have their current HPs even if this is above max. If healing such a character, he will lose any extra HPs.
- The eniripsa itself will not gain any extra maximum HP.
