# Revitalizing Word

This spell heals multiple allies in nearby area including self.

**Class:** Eniripsa

**Cost:** 300K

**AP:** 6

**Target:** Self

**Recast:** Yes

**Effect:** Heals 12 to targets nearby

**Critical Effect:** Heals 17 to targets nearby

**Area:** All tiles within distance 2 from and including caster tile. Totally 13 tiles affected.
