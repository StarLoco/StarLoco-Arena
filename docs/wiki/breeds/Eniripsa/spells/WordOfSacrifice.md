# Word of Sacrifice

This spell heals target ally while hurting caster.

**Class**: Eniripsa
**Cost**: 200K
**AP**: 3
**Target**: Non-Self
**Range**: 1 to 2
**Recast**: Yes
**Effect**: 12 neutral to self, heals 12 HP to target
**Critical Effect**: 15 neutral to self, heals 15 HP to target

---

**Notes**
- Even if the eniripsa has boosted healing (event card, bonus card or special tile), the spell will still hurt normal amount.
- Even if the eniripsa dies from this spell, the target will still be fully healed.
- This spell ignores line of sight.
