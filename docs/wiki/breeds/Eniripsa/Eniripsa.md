# Eniripsa's Hand

The eniripsa is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in healing. Initial stats: HP: 60, Initiative: 50

## Primary uses

- To increase AP of another target with one. In many teams the use of [Stimulating Word](./spells/StimulatingWord.md) is needed for the tactic to work. Some uses the eniripsa as a killing machine, boosting its AP to 9 with items and [stimulating word](./spells/StimulatingWord.md).
- The eniripsa is the only class that has the ability to heal with the sole exception of the sacrier spell [Life Transfer](../Sacrier/spells/LifeTransfer.md). Healing is especially useful for characters with [resistance](../../mechanics/Resistance.md), [damage rebound](../../mechanics/DamageRebound.md) and teams with a lot of mobility and control.
- The spell [Altruism](./spells/Altruism.md) is essential in many strategies. It heals all character including opponent. It is especially useful in strategies where the team does focused damage in a short period of time. It is also a very effective counter weapon against area effect damage.
- [Word of Youth](./spells/WordOfYouth.md) is the only spell that can dispel effect from friendly characters. Very useful to remove harmful debuffs such as [Slow Down](../Xelor/spells/SlowDown.md), [Rotten Luck](../Ecaflip/spells/RottenLuck.md) and [Roulette](../Ecaflip/spells/Roulette.md) to mention a few as well as harmful [event cards](../../rules/EventCards.md). Potentially very useful but very circumstantial.
