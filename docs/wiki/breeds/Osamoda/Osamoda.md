# Osamodas' Whip

The osamoda is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in summons that deal damage. Initial stats: [HP:](../../mechanics/HealthPoints.md) 70 [Initiative:](../../mechanics/Initiative.md) 70

## Primary uses

* The osamoda class is very specialized in summoning creatures. It is the only class able to summon creatures that do consistent damage. (Although the sadida's [doll](../Sadida/spells/TheSacrificialDoll.md) do more damage and the sram's [double](../Sram/spells/Double.md) have more HP.)
* It has some potent cheap buffs [Bear's Cry](./spells/BearsCry.md) and [High-energy Shot](./spells/HighEnergyShot.md) that can only be used against summons.
* The osamoda also has the most potent weapon against enemy summons: [Whip](./spells/Whip.md). (Though the spell is mostly used against your own badly placed summons.)
