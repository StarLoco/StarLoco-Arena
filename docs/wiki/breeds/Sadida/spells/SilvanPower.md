# Silvan Power

This spell transforms caster into an invulnerable, immobilized tree.

**Class:** Sadida

**Cost:** 100K

**AP:** 4

**Target:** Self

**Duration:** 3 Rounds

**Recast:** 10 Rounds

**Effect:** +100 dmg reduction of all Element, immobilization

---

**Notes**
- Works as spell [Immunity](../../Feca/spells/Immunity.md) plus the additional effect of [Immobilization](../../../mechanics/Immobilization.md).
