# The Sacrificial Doll

This spell summons a victim.

**Class**: Sadida
**Cost**: 250K
**AP**: 4
**Target**: Empty
**Range**: 1 to 1
**Recast**: 2 Rounds
**Effect**: Summons a victim

**Victim stats**

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 10 |
| MP | 2 |
| AP | 6 |

**Spell: Sacrifice**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| AP Cost | 4 |
| Range | 1 to 1 |
| Recast | 1 per turn |
| Effect | 30 neutral dmg, -50 neutral dmg self |
| Critical Effect | - |
| Behavior | Charge in |

---

**Notes**
- After casting its spell, the victim will self destruct.
- Damage is boosted by the special +5 neutral dmg tile.
- Casting Immunity, Truce or Sacrifice on a victim will save the victim from self destructing.
- See the Summons page for general information about summons.
