# Poisoning

This is a damage spell that will hurt target over time.

**Class**: [Sadida](../Sadida.md)

**Cost**: 200K

**AP**: 3

**Target**: Enemy

**Range**: 1 to 3

**Duration**: 3 Rounds

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: +3 Poisoning (3 (Neutral) dmg every round)

---

**Notes**

* This spell does the opposite of the spell [Regenerating Word](../../Eniripsa/spells/RegeneratingWord.md).
* The damage is inflicted at the beginning of the target's turn. No damage is taken at time when casting.
* Totally after 3 rounds, it will have hurt 18 damage if cast twice one round.
* See [this page](../../../mechanics/Damage.md) for a comparison chart of all damage spells.
