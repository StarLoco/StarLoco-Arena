# Sadida's Boots

The sadida is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in summons with control features. Initial stats: [HP:](../../mechanics/HealthPoints.md) 70 [Initiative:](../../mechanics/Initiative.md) 40

## Primary uses

- The defining spell of the sadida is probably [Madoll](./spells/Madoll.md). This spell acts both as an AP remover as well as a blocker which the enemy must waste AP to kill off. Often seen used together with items that boost the AP to 8 so that the sadida can cast [The Sacrificial Doll](./spells/TheSacrificialDoll.md), [Brambles](./spells/Brambles.md) or [Madoll](./spells/Madoll.md) twice each turn.
- [The Sacrificial Doll](./spells/TheSacrificialDoll.md) is another reason to use a sadida in your team. The doll inflicts 30 damage but maximum only once every third turn. Potentially deadly with the right strategy, but in average it does no more than other damage spells.
- The sadida also has to its disposal, the only damage spell that can affect all players simultaneously, (not counting [All or Nothing](../Ecaflip/spells/AllOrNothing.md) that only can be cast once), namely [Shaking](./spells/Shaking.md). Together with some kind of healing, many players have tried stomp enemy to death from a safe distance.
