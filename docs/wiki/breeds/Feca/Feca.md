# Feca

The feca is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in defensive, protective spells. Initial stats: HP: 65, Initiative: 45

## Primary uses

- As a tank with increased resistance spells it is potentially the best class to withstand damage.
- Its spell Bubble is one of the most potent long range spells. Awkward to use because of its fixed range but deadly because of its low AP cost.
- The feca is the only class that has access to the strategic useful spells Immunity and Truce.
