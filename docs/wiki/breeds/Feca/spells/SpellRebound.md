# Spell Rebound

This buff makes every incoming spell reflect with 5% chance to character who cast spell.

**Class:** Feca

**Cost:** 200K

**AP:** 6

**Target:** Self

**Duration:** Game

**Recast:** No

**Effect:** +5% spell rebound bonus

---

**Notes**
- A spell that is reflected is reflected to caster without affecting the feca.
- In addition of getting 5% chance of reflecting every incoming spell, the feca also enters reflecting state with 5% chance at the start of each round. This is seen as a small animation around the feca. In reflecting state, the feca will reflect the single next incoming spell with 100% certainty.
