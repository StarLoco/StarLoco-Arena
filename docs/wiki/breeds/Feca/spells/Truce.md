# Truce

This spell gives all characters immunity against most kinds of damage for one turn.

**Class**: Feca
**Cost**: 100K
**AP**: 3
**Target**: All
**Duration**: 1 Round
**Recast**: 10 Rounds
**Effect**: +100 dmg red. of all elements

---

**Notes**
- Similar to Immunity, except it targets all characters, enemy as ally.
- For further information see Immunity.
