# Weakness

This debuff decreases damage dealt of targeted enemies.

**Class**: Feca
**Cost**: 150K
**AP**: 1
**Target**: Enemy
**Range**: 1 to 6
**Duration**: 1 Round
**Recast**: Once Each
**Effect**: -3 dmg of all elements
**Critical Effect**: -5 dmg of all elements

---

**Notes**
- Together with spells like Earth and Water Armor, Wind and Fire Armor, Feca Shield and items with resistances, this spell can make the feca almost invincible. However he won't be able to deal out much damage.
