# Retreat Arrow

This is a damage spell which also pushes back target two tiles.

**Class:** Cra

**Cost:** 300K

**AP:** 4

**Target:** Enemy

**Range:** 2 to 5 in Line

**Recast:** Yes

**Effect:** 5 neutral dmg, pushes back target 2 tiles

**Critical Effect:** 8 neutral dmg, pushes back target 4 tiles

---

**Notes**
- If the target is pushed into an obstacle, it takes extra damage. The exact amount can be found [here](../../../mechanics/Damage.md).
- See [this page](../../../mechanics/Damage.md) for a comparison chart of all damage spells.
