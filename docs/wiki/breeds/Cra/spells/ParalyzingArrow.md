This is a damage spell which also have a chance to [immobilize](../../../mechanics/Immobilization.md) target.

**Class**: [Cra](../Cra.md)

**Cost**: 200K

**AP**: 5

**Target**: Enemy

**Range**: 2 to 6

**Duration**: 1 Round

**Recast**: Yes

**Effect**: 5 (Neutral) dmg, 10% chance of immobilization

**Critical Effect**: 8 (Neutral) dmg, 50% chance of immobilization
