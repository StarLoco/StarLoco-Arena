# Cra's Range

The cra is one of many [classes](../../rules/Classes.md). It specializes in [range](../../mechanics/Range.md). Initial stats: HP: 65, Initiative: 45

## Primary uses

- Is primarly used as a long range damage dealer. Maybe most successfully so with the [Homing Arrow](./spells/HomingArrow.md) which have the longest base range of all damage spells.
- Also frequently used as a part control, part damage unit with the excellent spell [Frozen Arrow](./spells/FrozenArrow.md).
