# Event Cards

At the beginning of each [round](../mechanics/Round.md), before any [character](./Characters.md) makes his turn, a random _event card_ is drawn. The effect is typically affecting all [characters](./Characters.md) and last an entire [round](../mechanics/Round.md) until next card is drawn. Each card turn up at most once. 12 of the cards, one for each [class](./Classes.md), are such that it gives a positive effect to a specific [class](./Classes.md) while it may (or may not) give a negative effect to [characters](./Characters.md) of other [classes](./Classes.md).
