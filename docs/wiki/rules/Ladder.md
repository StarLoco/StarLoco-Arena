# Ladder System

Dofus Arena have a simple _ladder system_ where one get or loses ladder experience every time you win or lose a match. This experience in turn determines a player's _XP level_ as from the table below.

| Level | Experience |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0-99 |
| 2 | 100-199 |
| 3 | 200-399 |
| 4 | 400-599 |
| 5 | 600-899 |
| 6 | 900-1199 |
| 7 | 1200-1599 |
| 8 | 1600-1999 |
| 9 | 2000-2499 |
| 10 | 2500-2999 |
| 11 | 3000-3499 |
| 12 | 3500-3999 |
| 13 | 4000-4499 |
| 14 | 4500-4999 |
| 15 | 5000-5499 |
| 16 | 5500-5999 |
| ... | and so on... |

The amount of ladder experience gained or lost depend somehow on the rank levels of the players and number of victories and defeats, but the exact formula is not yet determined.

XP Level is the level based on XP (the one you see in game), not the rank number. You need to check your and your opponents info before and after the match by searching after your and your opponent's name [here](http://www.dofus-arena.com/ladder/ladder.php) (offsite link). Note that it is not updated immediately, you might have to wait for a while. **Make sure only one fight has taken place for both of you.**

The in-game effect of the ladder system is only concerning the matching of players.
