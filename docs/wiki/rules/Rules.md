# General Rules

Dofus Arena is a turn-based strategic online game. It can be played in a couple different [modes](./Modes.md) but most common is the 1vs1 ladder game. Before the actual fight begins, each player must create his own [team](./Team.md) by spending 5000 [kamas](../mechanics/Kamas.md) (points). These points can be divided into buying [characters](./Characters.md), [spells](./Spells.md) and [items](../items/Items.md). [Characters](./Characters.md) can be chosen from 12 different [classes](./Classes.md), and for each class there are a set of available class-specific [spells](./Spells.md). Before starting a fight, one must choose a team-leader. (Note: the wiki's dedicated "team-leader" page was removed from the source; see [Team](./Team.md) and [BonusCards](./BonusCards.md) for related rules.)

At the beginning of a match, a [map](./Maps.md) is randomly chosen from 14 available. At the first phase of the fight both teams place their [characters](./Characters.md) as they see fit, but restricted to a number of highlighted tiles. After this 1-3 random [bonus cards](./BonusCards.md) are distributed to each team leader.

The fight are then divided into [rounds](../mechanics/Round.md). At the start of each [round](../mechanics/Round.md) a random [event card](./EventCards.md) which affects the whole [round](../mechanics/Round.md) is drawn. After that every [character](./Characters.md) of both [teams](./Team.md) gets to move and cast spells limited by their [movement points](../mechanics/MovementPoints.md) and [action points](../mechanics/ActionPoints.md). Each [character](./Characters.md) must be played before a timer goes out after which the turn goes on to the next one in turn. When within the [round](../mechanics/Round.md) the [characters](./Characters.md) get to play, is determined by the [character](./Characters.md)'s [initiative](../mechanics/Initiative.md).

The fight is ended when one of the [teams](./Team.md) has lost all his [characters](./Characters.md) or when one of the players resigns after which [ladder experience points](./Ladder.md) are distributed.
