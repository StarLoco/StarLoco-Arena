# Elements

[Damage](./Damage.md), weapons, [spells](../rules/Spells.md) and [resistances](./Resistance.md) may have one of following element attributes:

- Earth
- Fire
- Air
- Water

A [weapon](../items/Items.md#weapons) or a [spell](../rules/Spells.md) of a specific element only cause elemental damage of that element and is only modified by correspondent elemental modifier. Thus it is important to consider which spell or weapon you are going to use when choosing items for boosting damage. Similarly you have different [resistances](./Resistance.md) for different elements.

Some spells lack element and is considered neutral.

Neutral damage typically can not be boosted nor resisted. However there are some exceptions: [Immunity](../breeds/Feca/spells/Immunity.md), [Truce](../breeds/Feca/spells/Truce.md), [Silvan Power](../breeds/Sadida/spells/SilvanPower.md) all makes target immune against most neutral damage. See [this page](../breeds/Feca/spells/Immunity.md) for exceptions.

There are also a special tile that gives +5 Neutral damage. See [this page](./Damage.md) for more information which spells are boosted by this spell.
