# Round

A fight is divided into rounds. Each round consist of the following:

- An [event-card](../rules/EventCards.md) is drawn
- The [character](../rules/Characters.md) with highest initiative plays
- The [character](../rules/Characters.md) with next highest initiative plays, and so on until...
- The [character](../rules/Characters.md) with lowest initiative plays

Characters with equal initiative get their turn order randomly determined once before the game starts.

The game last 15 normal rounds after which [tiles starts dropping](./TileDrop.md).
