# Levels

OBS! This page is currently obsolete. In the latest version of Dofus Arena, characters does not gain XP or levels.

Old rules regarding levels:

When winning a fight, all surviving characters get 10 experience points. Dead characters that haven't been [lethally killed](./LethalBlows.md) is granted 5 experience points. If you have 10 consecutive victories, you are instead granted 15 experience points for survived characters and 7 for dead characters.

Losing a fight yields no gain or loss of experience points.

When collected enough experience points, characters gain levels according to the table below:

| Level Name | Experience |
|---|---|
| Neophyte | 0-90 |
| Beginner | 100-249 |
| Advanced | 250-449 |
| Warrior | 450-699 |
| Veteran | 700-999 |
| Champion | 1000-1499 |
| Star | 1500-1999 |
| God of the Arena | 2000-2999 |
| Legend | 3000 |

For each new level the character gets a random [skill](../rules/Skills.md).

The total amount of levels you and your opponent have determine how many [bonus cards](../rules/BonusCards.md) are dealt at the beginning of the fight.
