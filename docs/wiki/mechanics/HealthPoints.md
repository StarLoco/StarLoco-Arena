# HealthPoints

## Health Points

Every character has a maximum and current value of _health points_ (HP). When a character's HP reaches 0 a character die and in some cases may become permanent dead (see Lethal Blows). A character can never have more HP than its maximum value, although this maximum value can be altered by certain items and spells.

A character's initial HP depends on class:

| Feca | Osamoda | Enutrof | Sram | Xelor | Ecaflip | Eniripsa | Iop | Cra | Sadida | Sacrier | Panda |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 65 | 70 | 65 | 70 | 65 | 75 | 60 | 75 | 65 | 70 | 80 | 75 |
