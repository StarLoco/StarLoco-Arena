# Lethal Blows

OBS! In the latest version of Dofus Arena, lethal blows have no significance due to the fact that characters does not gain XP or levels.

Every time a character dies, it has a 2% chance of being the subject of a _lethal blow_, a permanent death. After such an event, the [character](../rules/Characters.md) loses all his [XP](./Levels.md) points and [levels](./Levels.md), the character slot gets greyed out and is rendered unusable until the character is removed and a new one created. The percentage of landing a lethal blow can be altered by a certain [bonus card](../rules/BonusCards.md).

It is known that most kinds of deaths can be lethal, but the exact circumstances are not yet fully explored. Below are a table of which circumstances are certain can lead to a lethal death:

| Circumstance | Can it be lethal? |
|---|---|
| Getting killed by opponent damage. | Yes. |
| Getting killed by giving up. | No. |
| Getting killed by standing on a tile which are [dropped](./TileDrop.md). | Unknown. |
| Getting killed by self-inflicting damage spells, such as [Mutilation](../breeds/Iop/spells/Mutilation.md). | Yes. |
| Getting killed by standing on a harmful [special tile](./Tiles.md). | Yes. |
| Getting killed by walking down a height | Yes. |
| Getting killed by being pushed into an obstacle. | Yes. |
| Getting killed by being pushed or thrown into a trap. | Yes. |
