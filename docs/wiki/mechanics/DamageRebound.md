# Damage Rebound

A character with damage rebound will bounce back damage every time he get hit. Unlike [damage reflection](./DamageReflect.md) he will still take the full damage. For example assume someone hit a character with +5 damage rebound with a 20 dmg spell. The caster will hurt 5, the target will hurt 20.

There are currently two items that will give a damage rebound buff:

| Item | Cost | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Prespic Lining | 150K | +2 dmg rebound |
| Prespic Cloak | 200K | +3 dmg rebound |
