# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

_Note: This is a strictly non-official FAQ._

**Q:** What is negative AP and how is it used?

**A:** Spells such as [Slow Down](../breeds/Xelor/spells/SlowDown.md) removes AP from opponent hindering his capability to cast spells.

**Q:** How do my characters get XP and increase levels?

**A:** In the current version, they don't. We are probably going to see a new system of character development in the next version.

**Q:** What are weapons used for? / How do I use weapons?

**A:** Weapons are a substitute for spells that all classes can use. You can have both weapon and spells but you can only use one at the time. Read about weapons [here](../items/Items.md).

**Q:** How do I increase my damage?

**A:** Choose equipment that boost the correct element of the weapon or spell you are using. Read about elements [here](../mechanics/Elements.md), and read about items [here](../items/Items.md).

**Q:** My opponent doesn't move and just sits there doing nothing. What is going on?

**A:** It could be connection related. If this is the case the opponent will usually eventually automatically be booted. It could also be a bug that makes one of the side unable to move and chat. If this is the case, the opponent are still able to resign which is usually what happens. The last possibility is that opponent is waiting for you to resign to get easy ladder points, maybe even using a script. (Note that using a third party program is against the User Agreement.)

**Q:** I have a mac and everything moves very sloppy especially when casting charge and in the end when tiles are dropping. What is wrong?

**A:** Unfortunately the mac version of flash player is not as fast as the windows version.

**Q:** How do I save a replay of the game? I have a mac.

**A:** Unfortunately that feature is not implemented in the mac version. (However you can watch replays.)

**Q:** Something in this wiki is incorrect. Can I change it?

**A:** Sure! If you are certain, go ahead. However remember that sometimes things are changed between versions. Be sure that you have recently tested it yourself before changing. If you don't have time to check it, leave a comment on the page in question.

**Q:** This wiki is great! Can I somehow help out?

To do this click on the little + in the bottom left corner. Check [this page](./To-Do.md) for things that need to be done. Only change things that you are certain of. Write hypothesis as comments to the page.

**Q:** I have an idea to what could be included in this wiki. What do I do?

**A:** Go ahead, write the article. If you wish something to be included, leave a comment at the front page or add a line on [this page](./To-Do.md).

**Q:** I have a question that is not on this list. What do I do?

**A:** Edit this page and add the question. Hopefully someone will answer it.

**Q:** how do you get items like clothes for your trainer and can you get items for characters that are not on the starting list of items?

**A:** You Get items by winning battles. although if the player resigns you will not get an item. the higher your ladder rating usually the better the item. you may not get an item if you rating is low.
